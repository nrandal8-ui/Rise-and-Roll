About Rise and Roll

Rise and Roll is an after-school enrichment program that comes to programs already serving students. Instead of asking families to find something new, we add skating and STEM to what your site already offers.

How a cohort runs

  • We come to you

    Sessions happen at your site, on a gym, cafeteria, or other indoor floor.

  • Skating and STEM

    One 1–2 hour session a week for 6–8 weeks, pairing a skating lesson with a hands-on STEM lesson.

  • Trained volunteers

    Background-checked volunteers teach in pairs. We bring skates, helmets, pads, and supplies.

  • A final showcase

    Students present a project and a performance matched to their skating level.
