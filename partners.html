Bring Rise and Roll to your program

We come to your site for a 6–8 week cohort that adds skating and STEM to what you already offer. Are you interested in hosting? Here's what it takes.

What you'll need

  • Space

    Basketball court, cafeteria floor or any indoor floor

  • Group size

    10-15 students maximum per cohort

  • Equipment

    We bring skates, helmets, pads, and STEM supplies.

  • Cohort length

    6–8 weeks, ending with a student showcase.

Frequently asked questions

Do students need their own skates?

No. Rise and Roll provides skates and safety gear in a range of sizes. If students have their own gear, they are welcomed to use that.

What if a student has never skated?

Most haven't. Sessions start with balance and safety, and showcase performances are matched to each student's level.

Who teaches the sessions?

Trained volunteer instructors lead the skating and STEM portions. Every volunteer completes a background check and orientation, and instructors teach in pairs, so each cohort of 10–15 students has at least two adults.
