Get involved

Rise and Roll runs on volunteers and supporters. Teach a skill, sponsor a cohort, or donate gear, and help more students learn to skate and build something they're proud of.

Volunteer roles

Volunteers teach in pairs, so you'll never lead a session alone.

  • Skating instructor

    Teach balance, gliding, stopping, and simple routines, matched to each student's level.

    • Comfortable on roller skates
    • Patient with beginners
    • Coaching experience is a plus, not required

  • STEM instructor

    Lead hands-on lessons that connect to skating, like forces and motion, and guide students' showcase projects.

    • Background or interest in science, tech, engineering, or math
    • Enjoys hands-on projects
    • Teaching experience is a plus, not required
